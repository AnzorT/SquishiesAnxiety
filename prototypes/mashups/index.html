Four mashups to try

Each idea crosses two kinds of games. Every box below is a small working example, so you can feel how each one plays before anything gets built into the app.

Box-pushing puzzle + soft bodiesMy pick

Squish Squeeze

Tuck everyone into bed. Walk into a gap that is too tight and you squeeze thin. The moment there is room, you puff back up and shove whatever is beside you. Toy blocks go into the dashed toy boxes. Blocks can't be pulled, and a round plush can't push a block into a narrow gap because its other half hits the wall. So the puzzle is planning where you squeeze, and where you pop out.

Moves 0Best possible 0

Arrow keys, swipe, or tap where you want to go. Three stars for the best possible move count, two for up to half as many again (or if you used a hint). Hint works out your next move from where you are, and tells you if you are stuck.

Claw machine + roguelite

Claw Run

Three machines in a row. Win enough plushies on each one before your tries run out, then pick an upgrade for the rest of the run. Each machine needs one more win than the last, and its claw swings faster.

Match-3 + rotating gravity

Wash Day

The squad is smelly. Spin the drum and the plushies tumble. When three of the same colour touch, they come out clean. Every spin uses water, so plan your turns.

Clean 0/12 Water

Drag around the drum, or hold a spin button. In the app this would hang off the Crib: washing is how you fix a smelly plush.

Rhythm game + memory game

Squish Choir

The choir sings a short phrase. Play it back with the same singers and the same rhythm, off-beats included. Every round is longer and faster, and twists arrive: two singers swap seats, two sing at once, or they hide behind a curtain so you can only hear them. One wrong singer or one off-beat tap fails the round.

Round 1 Tries 3 Score 0 92 BPM Turn your sound on. Tap the singers to learn their voices, then press Start.

Tap a singer, or press 1 to 5 for the seats. After a swap the keys still mean seats, not singers. Each singer has their own sound, which you need for the curtain rounds. Bubbles joins in round 3: one more voice to tell apart.

These are rough playable sketches. The plushies are drawn stand-ins and the sounds are made on the fly. In the app they would be your rig creatures with their real squish sounds, plus the usual levels, tiers and XP.
